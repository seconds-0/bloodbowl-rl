# Training audit, 2026-08-20: why the obs-v6 lineage does not score

Requested by Alex (2026-08-20 21:43 PDT): "rigorously audit our training profile,
our weights, our rewards. Research everything, take NOTHING for granted."

Method: eight independent adversarial auditors, each with one lens (reward
semantics, observation/action encoding, network + optimizer + schedule,
self-play and bank row masking, episode structure and curriculum, empirical
rig-log telemetry, a skeptical read of D1-D251, and engine rules with
bot-vs-bot / random probes). Every load-bearing claim below was then
re-verified by the session against the source that runs
(`puffer/bloodbowl/bloodbowl.h`, `engine/src`, the PufferLib CUDA trainer at
`vendor/PufferLib/src/*.cu` plus `training/*.patch`) or against the rig logs.
Claims are labelled CONFIRMED (file:line or measured) or SUSPECTED.

`1787193752314`) scores 0.083-0.088 TD/game vs the contact bot from kickoff
and concedes 0.99-1.03 (D250). The seed-42 replicate with one bot-bank rung

### F3. CONFIRMED: the optimizer is Muon; LR is a relative step and ours is tiny
`vendor/PufferLib/src/pufferlib.cu:2051` calls `muon_init`, not Adam.
`src/muon.cu:178-243`: gradient clipped to `max_grad_norm`, Nesterov momentum
0.95, per-matrix Frobenius normalisation, 5 Newton-Schulz iterations, then
`w -= lr * sqrt(max(1, R/C)) * orth(G)`. All five parameter tensors are 2-D,
so every update has the same spectral size regardless of gradient magnitude:
`lr` IS the per-step relative change, and `max_grad_norm`, `vf_coef`,
`ent_coef` only rotate the direction. Reference points: `config/default.ini`
0.015, `config/chess.ini` (swept) 0.000573, ours 0.00028 (from a 16-trial
200M-step fine-tune sweep on the June league lineage whose search floor was
1e-4, D137), chained rungs 2.8e-5. Nothing about this optimizer is mentioned
in DECISIONS.md or the docs; every LR discussion assumed Adam.

### F4. CONFIRMED: `replay_ratio 0.25` means 2 gradient steps per 131k-sample epoch
`src/pufferlib.cu:1573`: `total_minibatches = replay_ratio * batch_size /
minibatch_size = 0.25 * 131072 / 16384 = 2`. `update_epochs` appears nowhere
in `src/` or `pufferlib/*.py` (the CLI accepts and ignores it). Minibatches
are 256 segments sampled with replacement by prioritised advantage from 1,560
eligible learner rows; expected unique coverage <= 28% per epoch. A 5B rung
is 76k small Muon steps on about a quarter of its data.

### F5. CONFIRMED: the reward is 94% shaping and the touchdown is a net-negative step
Rung-1 per-episode signed component sums (team 0): `distance_ball` -0.33,
`block_sequence` -0.12, `distance_endzone` -0.085, `block_turnover` -0.073,
`rush` -0.017, `ball_loss` -0.008, `block_exposure` +0.059, `ball_gain`
+0.035, `result_winloss` +0.028, `possession` +0.023, `touchdown` **+0.017**.
TD + win = 0.049 of a signed mass of 0.77 (6%). Advantages are normalised per
minibatch (`pufferlib.cu:812`), so shaping variance sets the scale the
objective is measured against.

At the TD step `bloodbowl.h:3912-3916` pays +0.4 and `:3985-4005` emits the
exact PBRS term `gamma*Phi(s') - Phi(s)`; `proc_match.c:947-948` sets the
ball OFF_PITCH in the same apply, so Phi_carry(s') = 0 and the step nets
`0.4 - 0.04*(25 - d_prev)`. Phi_carry at d=1 is 0.96, i.e. larger than the TD
it shapes. Harness measurement (reward auditor, contact bot vs contact bot,
161 TDs): TD-step reward to the scorer **-0.557 mean, negative in 161/161**.
PBRS is invariant only if the critic represents V - Phi exactly; with a
potential twice the size of the true value of the state, any critic
smoothing makes "advance" look better than "finish". Holding the ball leaks
`-(1-gamma)*Phi` per c-step: -0.32 per turn-pair at d=1.

Game-theoretically (reward auditor), against an opponent that scores back,
"score now" and "score on turn 8" differ by ~0.03 at gamma 0.995, below
shaping noise, and the possession annuity (+0.03 per held turn-end, 16
coupons = 0.48 > one TD) pushes toward stalling. `reward_draw` is 0.

### F6. CONFIRMED: the base policy sits in a non-play equilibrium
Rung-1 eval (10k games, both teams summed): 1.25 pickup attempts, 0.66
successful, possession 0.15, 0.0011 passes, 0 handoffs, 8.4 blocks thrown
(humans ~40 per team), 5 GFIs, 64% draws, 0.48 TD. Possessions advance 3.1
squares. In the exam the contact bot rushes ~65 times and falls ~11 times per
game and the champion converts that into 0.02-0.06 TDs and a 1-2% win rate:
it never gets a carrier into scoring range even when handed the ball. The
July policy in the same exam: 6.2 pickups, possession 0.32, 18 blocks.

### F7. CONFIRMED: training `tds` is a both-sides mixture and cannot gate anything
`bloodbowl.h:3002-3007`: `tds += d0 + d1`, summed over mirror envs (52% of
games), three frozen banks (36%) and the bot bank (12%); the bot's own TDs
contribute ~0.12 of the reported 0.75. `perf` is slot-0 win rate, ~0.5 by
symmetry in mirror games. The same-start calibration (episode auditor): two
contact bots score 0.88/episode on rung-6 starts; the rung-6 checkpoints
accepted at tds 0.30 were below a trivial bot on the same starts. The D168
plateau rule and every rung acceptance in D235-D251 were read on this metric.

### F8. CONFIRMED: opponent shares, rotation, and sides
`frozen_bank_pct` is a row fraction; each historical env consumes one frozen
row and one learner row, so with 4 banks at 0.06 the launcher's own print
says `historical_game_share = 244/512 = 0.477`. Learner-row shares: mirror
68.7%, anchor 7.8%, kept-1 7.8%, kept-2 7.8%, bot 7.8%. Banks never rotate
within a rung (`run_reward_ablation.sh:785-788` forces `swap_winrate 1.1`,
`opp_timeout 10x STEPS`, `snapshot_interval 1e12`; the ini's selfplay values
are dead). The learner is slot 0 = HOME against every real opponent and plays
AWAY only in mirror envs; obs are egocentric and x-mirrored (0 failures in an
8.2M-round-trip probe) so this is low impact. Row masking, frozen forward
pass, tag arithmetic, scripted-bank exclusion and the exam harness were all
verified correct.

### F9. CONFIRMED: recurrent state regime differs between training and exam
`src/bindings.cu:143-153` zeroes MinGRU state for every rollout
(`reset_state=1`, required for training by `puffer_recurrent_eval_state.patch`);
`pufferlib.cu:1598` zeroes the BPTT initial state. Memory in training is at
most 63 env steps (~32 own decisions, about one team turn) and is never reset
at episode boundaries. The exam's eval phase carries state for the whole game
(up to 4096 steps) and resets at terminals. Every scoreboard number is from a
hidden-state regime the policy never trained in. Cheap check: compare the
train-phase windows (LR 1e-12, reset every 64) with the eval-phase windows
in any existing exam log.

### F10. CONFIRMED: representation is hostile to the task
Encoder = single bias-free `puf_mm` on raw bytes (`src/models.cu:412-417`,
`kernels.cu:341-346`), no normalisation, no per-env encoder. Into it go skill
ids (1..108) as magnitudes in sorted slots, a packed bitfield byte (USED=1,
ACTIVATING=2, HAS_BALL=8, ...), every position as scalar (x+1, y+1) bytes,
and probability planes 0..255. There is no ball plane, carrier plane or
occupancy plane, while the 391-way square head is spatially indexed. Mean
obs L2 norm 271 (620 on MOVE windows). The docs' own mitigations
(policy-research.md:320-325) were never implemented. Judgment item, but the
June BC work (D172) already showed a 0.45 val-exact ceiling bound by
"model/recipe", not data.

### F11. CONFIRMED: credit horizon
gamma 0.995, lambda 0.85 per c-step, both agents step every decision.
GAE direct window 1/(1-gamma*lambda) = 6.5 steps; critic horizon 200 steps,
half-life 138. Measured first-pickup to TD: 179 c-steps mean (max 618) in
bot-vs-bot, 84 vs random; a kickoff drive is 80-180 c-steps. A TD 300 steps
out is worth 0.09 and reaches the key decision only through the critic. The
gamma ladder proposed in `docs/reward-audit-decision-time.md:104-118` was
never run; every run in history used gamma in [0.9948, 0.9976], lambda 0.85.

## 2. What this means

The obs-v6 lineage failed for compounding reasons, none of them "needs more
curriculum": an optimizer nobody knew was Muon run 2-50x below its reference
step size and then scaled down another 10x, two gradient steps per epoch, a
reward whose shaping terms outweigh the objective 16:1 and make the
touchdown step itself negative, and a training metric that could not see any
of it. The strongest policy the project owns was trained in June/July from a
BC prior on one kickoff config for ~270B steps, and the tooling, not an
experiment, retired it.
